+ Proficiency in data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms.

+ Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret complex datasets and trends.

+ Experience in creating clear, actionable, and visually effective charts and graphs.

+ Ability to communicate technical insights to non-technical stakeholders.

+ Familiarity with statistical analysis and data interpretation methodologies.

+ Experience with SQL or other data querying languages is a plus.

+ Attention to detail and a methodical approach to problem-solving.
