The Role

As a Data Analyst at Air Apps, you will be responsible for analyzing and
interpreting data to drive business insights and optimize product performance.
You will create reports, dashboards, and performance metrics to help teams
make informed decisions and improve user experience.

This role requires a data-driven mindset, strong analytical skills, and
experience with visualization tools to turn complex data into actionable
insights.

e This is a fully onsite position, based at our office in Lisbon, where you will
collaborate closely with cross-functional teams in person and contribute
to a dynamic and fast-paced environment. We are open to support with
relocation efforts.

Responsibilities
e Analyze user behavior, product performance, and key business metrics
to identify trends and opportunities.

e Create and maintain interactive dashboards and reports using tools like
Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or Google Data Studio.

e Collect, clean, and structure large datasets to ensure accuracy and
usability.

e Define and track KPIs for marketing, product, and business performance.
e Work with SQL, Python, or R to extract, analyze, and manipulate data.

e Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and marketing teams to
provide data-driven insights.

e Conduct A/B testing and experiment analysis to improve product
features and user engagement.

e Identify bottlenecks and opportunities in user journeys and customer
funnels.

e Present findings in a clear and compelling way to stakeholders and
leadership teams.

e Stay up to date with industry trends, analytics best practices, and
emerging data tools.

Requirements

e Around 3+ years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, or a
related field.

e Proficiency in SQL for data querying and manipulation.

e Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI,
Google Data Studio).

e Strong analytical skills with experience in Python or R for data analysis.
e Knowledge of A/B testing methodologies and statistical analysis.

e Experience with Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar user
analytics tools.

e Strong problem-solving skills and ability to communicate insights to non-
technical stakeholders.

e Experience working with large datasets and cloud-based data
warehouses (BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake).

e Familiarity with ETL processes and data pipeline optimization is a plus.

e Ability to work in a fast-paced, data-driven environment and manage
multiple projects.
