# Sagaci Research: Senior Data Analyst, Consumer Quantitative Research Team (from May 2026, Barcelona)

Sagaci Research offered Brenda the position of Senior Data Analyst in its Consumer Quantitative Research Team, with a start date of 18 May 2026. The office is in Poblenou, Barcelona, with hybrid working.

## Responsibilities in the role

- Product development (data and technology): develop new data structures and interfaces for internal and direct client interaction with the product; with the SagaBrand and SagaCube managers, define data content needs, analysis needs and reporting infrastructure.
- Data analysis process: own, recommend and enable an efficient, high-quality process for quantitative data analysis adapted to specific research methodologies and modeling.
- Build the tools and training that help the research and scripting teams collaborate, and serve as the reference for project delivery.
- Statistical validation: guide the research team in applying advanced statistical methods and support regression, drivers and segmentation modeling.
- Dashboard development: design and maintain interactive dashboards for internal needs and direct client data interaction.
- Research collaboration: translate researchers' objectives into technical requirements and develop custom metrics in the database for deeper analysis.
- Act as a day-to-day coach for Scripting and Data Analysts to grow the company's data expertise.
