# BeautifulSpreadsheets (2024)

Mini-projects built on the catalogue of data behind more than 130 Information is Beautiful infographics (topics include plastic waste, large language models and Marvel movies).

## Mini-projects

- Anomaly detection: find unusual patterns or possible inaccuracies in the data behind a publication (completed November 2024, then generalised).
- Automated data-storytelling generator: use NLP to extract key insights and trends from the datasets and generate narratives.
- Data quality assessment tool: assess the completeness and quality of the catalogue's datasets.
- Time-series analysis of how topics evolve: cancelled.
