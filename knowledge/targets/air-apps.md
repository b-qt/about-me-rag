Brenda saved two Air Apps postings: Data Analyst and Data Engineer, both in the Platform Data/Analytics Squad. The listing header says Madrid, remote, full time; the description says the role is fully onsite in Lisbon with relocation support.

## About the company

Air Apps is a family-founded company born in Lisbon in 2018, with offices in Lisbon and San Francisco. It is building an AI-powered Personal & Entrepreneurial Resource Planner, is self-funded and has more than 100 million downloads.

## Responsibilities

- Analyse user behaviour, product performance and key business metrics to identify trends and opportunities.
- Create and maintain interactive dashboards and reports (Tableau, Looker, Power BI or Google Data Studio).
- Collect, clean and structure large datasets.
- Define and track KPIs for marketing, product and business performance.
- Use SQL, Python or R to extract, analyse and manipulate data.
- Work with product managers, engineers and marketing teams.
- Run A/B tests and experiment analysis.
- Find bottlenecks and opportunities in user journeys and funnels, and present findings to stakeholders and leadership.

## Requirements

- Around 3+ years in data analysis, business intelligence or a related field.
- SQL proficiency; Python or R for analysis.
- Data visualisation tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI, Google Data Studio).
- A/B testing methodology and statistical analysis.
- Product analytics tools such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel or Amplitude.
- Large datasets and cloud warehouses (BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake).
- ETL and pipeline optimisation is a plus.
