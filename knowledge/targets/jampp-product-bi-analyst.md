# TARGET ROLE (job posting, NOT past work): Jampp, Product BI Analyst

## About the company

Jampp is a programmatic advertising platform (founded 2013, part of the Affle Group) that uses machine learning and creative optimisation to drive growth for mobile advertisers such as Uber, Just Eat Takeaway.com, FREENOW, Rappi and BIGO Live.

## The role

Join a new deep-learning platform initiative (shared embeddings across prediction, bidding and pricing) from day one, as the analytical bridge between the Data Science and ML teams and the business: understand the data, monitor experiments, find which advertisers benefit from new models and where performance lags. Highly technical for BI, working with auction-level, high-cardinality data.

## Duties

- Keep monitoring and reporting scalable with automated SQL and Python pipelines.
- Partner with Data Scientists and ML Engineers: flag data-quality issues and validate results from a business angle.

## Requirements

- 4–8 years in data analytics, BI, marketing or growth analytics.
- Strong SQL on large datasets; working Python (pandas-level analysis).
- Looker and/or Tableau; cloud warehouses (BigQuery preferred).
- A/B testing and experimentation fundamentals (significance, sample size, basic causal inference).
- Understanding of the ad-tech ecosystem (auctions, impressions, SSPs, MMPs).
- Good English and Spanish.
- Nice to have: programmatic advertising experience; predictive modeling or basic ML.
