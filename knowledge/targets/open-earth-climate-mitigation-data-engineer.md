# TARGET ROLE (job posting, NOT past work): Open Earth Foundation, Climate Mitigation Data Engineer

## The role

Build the data cities use to plan, act on and track their climate goals: greenhouse-gas inventories, estimates of what each action could cut, and progress monitoring and reporting to frameworks such as the GHG Protocol for Cities (GPC) and CDP. Owns the mitigation methods, inputs and models; the Platform Data Engineer owns the shared warehouse. Mid-level role.

## Duties

- Design and maintain data models for emissions inventories, activity data, emission factors, sources and methodologies.
- Build pipelines that ingest national, global and city-provided datasets, with tests, versioning and lineage.
- Review emissions-calculation methodologies (logic, inputs, assumptions, global-warming-potential choices) and flag gaps.
- Encode reporting rules so outputs align with the GPC and other frameworks.
- Snapshot factors and methods onto results so historical inventories stay reproducible.
- Set data-quality checks, compare results against independent sources, document everything, and support cities with questions about their numbers.

## Qualifications

- 5+ years in data engineering or a closely related role.
- Strong Python and SQL; relational databases and schema design (e.g. PostgreSQL).
- Working knowledge of greenhouse-gas accounting frameworks (GPC or IPCC methods).
- An orchestration tool (dbt, Airflow, Dagster, Mage or similar); cloud platforms and data warehouses.
- Nice to have: building or reviewing emissions inventories, emission-factor catalogues, GPC reporting levels, CDP reporting, geospatial tools (PostGIS, GeoPandas).
