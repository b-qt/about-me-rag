# TARGET ROLE (job posting, NOT past work): BETWEEN, Data Engineer / Data Analyst (Sant Cugat del Vallès)

BETWEEN is an IT and engineering consultancy. The posting (in Spanish) is for a Data Engineer / Data Analyst in an international team in Sant Cugat del Vallès, working on analytics, reporting and data-management projects. Hybrid: 3 days a week in the office.

## Main duties

- Write and optimise complex SQL for data extraction and analysis.
- Work with large databases, ensuring data quality and consistency.
- Extract, clean and transform data from different sources; design and maintain data pipelines.
- Automate processes with Python.
- Build and evolve Power BI dashboards and reports for stakeholders.
- Turn business needs into data solutions with multidisciplinary teams.

## Requirements

- 2–3 years in data, BI or analytics environments.
- Advanced English (B2–C1) for daily communication.
- Advanced SQL; Python for data processing; Power BI reporting; building and maintaining data pipelines.
